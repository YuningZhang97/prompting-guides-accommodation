*Note.* All three vendors, general and model guides. "Page taken down or merged": the page carrying the recommendation had no current version in the period of removal. Of recommendations that a current guide presented as advice to follow, 25.6% of machine-specific accommodation and 14.2% of general communication were later removed without notice. Of withdrawn machine-specific accommodation, 56% was explicitly retracted, compared with 31% of withdrawn general communication. Walk-backs (28 by the first coder) are excluded because of low reliability.
